ASIF NAWAZ MINHAS

Offensive Security Specialist

Red Teaming • Penetration Testing • Vulnerability Research

I am an Offensive Security Specialist with more than 10 years of hands-on experience in penetration testing, red teaming, vulnerability research, and enterprise security assessments.

My background spans web application testing, Windows and Active Directory security, infrastructure assessments, and adversary simulation, with a focus on identifying high-impact weaknesses, validating real-world risk, and helping organisations strengthen their overall security posture.

I also contribute to the security research and responsible disclosure community, with more than 600 reported security issues and 68 CVEs. My work combines technical depth with clear reporting, remediation guidance, and knowledge sharing to turn offensive security findings into meaningful defensive improvements.
